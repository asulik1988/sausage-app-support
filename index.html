How to Get Support

Need help with the Sausage Calculator app? Use our integrated Community Feedback feature to share ideas, report bugs, and get direct assistance from Adam. Your feedback helps make the app better for everyone!

Community Feedback feature in the Sausage Calculator app

Step-by-Step Guide

1
Open the App
Launch the Sausage Calculator app on your iOS or Android device. Make sure you have the latest version installed.
2
Navigate to Community Feedback
Look for "Community Feedback" in the app menu or settings. This feature is available to all users.
3
Browse Existing Feedback
• View trending requests and ideas from other users
• Filter by Bugs, Ideas, UX, or Closed items
• Upvote requests you support (you have 4 votes!)
4
Submit Your Feedback
Tap the Share new feedback button to submit your own idea, bug report, or question directly to Adam.
💡 Pro Tip for Better Support
When contacting support, please include:
  • A clear description of your issue or question
  • What you were trying to do when the problem occurred
  • Your device type (iPhone/Android) and app version
  • Screenshots if applicable (you can attach them in the message)
